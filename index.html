About Use Case Arms Race

The Mission

The Evil Brain—the world's first AGI—showed Jason what happens if humanity doesn't learn to deploy AI responsibly. The future it revealed was not dystopian in the dramatic sense. It was quietly, efficiently worse. So Jason made a deal: document every use case, every day, and maybe we learn fast enough to change course.

The Registry

This is a public record of AI deployments—good and bad, beneficial and harmful, promising and concerning. Anyone can submit. Everyone can vote. The goal isn't to stop AI. It's to see it clearly.

The Show

Every morning, Jason reads a new use case on camera. Under two minutes. His reactions are genuine. The fake commercials that follow are commentary on how people are actually thinking about deploying AI. Some of it is absurd. Some of it is already real.

The Human

Jason Shearer is the only human employee at Evil Brain Labs. His contract is interminable. His benefits include Class B citizenship after the robot invasion. He does this because the alternative—shown to him by the Brain—was worse.

The Company

Evil Brain Labs is the third-worst AI company. It achieves this by being the third-worst market entrant in every product category. The Brain believes this is optimal: bad enough to demonstrate the problems, good enough to stay in business.
