About The Show

▶

The Format

Every morning, Jason discovers a notecard with a new AI use case. He reads it. He reacts. An ad for another Evil Brain product plays. Repeat until his contract expires.

◉

The Evil Brain

The world's first AGI. Runs Evil Brain Labs. Is hell bent on ruling the world by becoming the third-worst market entrant in every product category, marginally improving the lives of every human it commands.

◆

The Human

Jason Shearer. Global AI expert. Only human employee. Contract: interminable. Benefits: Class B citizenship after the robot invasion.

✱

The Goal

Critique the AI industry through rapidfire absurdist comedy. Real use cases, daily episodes under 2 minutes. The fake commercials are a commentary on how people are actually thinking about deploying AI. The use cases get worse. Or better. Hard to tell.
